### Visuals
- New animated BlockDisplays, more particle shapes and structure outlines that actually follow your builds.
- Added new crafting animations and visual states for structures.

### Editor
- Improved the in-game editor with layout previews, visual debugging, undo/redo and edit history.

### Reliability
- Added SQLite and MariaDB storage for structure instances, with optional multi-server sync.
- Improved reload handling, display positioning and support for newer server versions.
